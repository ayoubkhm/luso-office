Understand before you choose.

Do I need to be a member to request a service?+

No. You can choose a one-off service without joining. Membership gives access to member prices.

Are services included in the membership?+

No. Visits, administrative support and other services are chosen and charged separately from membership.

How is the price confirmed?+

Catalogue prices are indicative. The scope, VAT, travel and external costs are detailed in the proposal before you agree.

Which costs need to be distinguished?+

LusoOffice services, official fees and external professionals’ fees are itemised in the proposal.

What should I include in the first conversation?+

Your needs, the location, steps already taken and your timeline. These details help define the assignment.

What do you need to arrange in Portugal?

A home, paperwork or a return: tell us what brings you here.
